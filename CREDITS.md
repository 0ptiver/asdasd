# Credits

## Assets

All art, models, textures, sound effects and music are **generated procedurally at runtime** (primitive geometry with vertex colors, WebAudio synthesis). No CC0 or third-party media files are bundled.

## Inspiration

Gameplay loop inspired by _Lumber Tycoon 2_ (Roblox). No code, models, textures, sounds or branded item names from that game are used; all names, layouts and content here are original.
